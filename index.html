Privacy Policy

App: Arrow Swipe | Last updated: November 2026

Welcome to Arrow Swipe ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy outlines how our mobile application handles user information and data when you download and play Arrow Swipe.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Arrow Swipe is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or ask for any personally identifiable personal information (PII) such as:

  • Your real name, phone number, or home address
  • Your email address or social media login details
  • Financial, banking, or credit card information
  • Your contacts, microphone, camera, or sensitive device sensors

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All core gameplay data is stored exclusively offline on your local device storage:

  • Gameplay Progress: Completed puzzle levels, highest scores, and star achievements.
  • Game Preferences: In-game audio volume, sound effects toggle, and board color themes.

This data stays on your phone and is never transmitted to any external private server.

3. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs

Arrow Swipe is a free casual puzzle game supported by standard mobile advertisements. We integrate Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads (such as optional video ads watched to unlock level hints).

Google AdMob & Google Play Services

Google AdMob may automatically collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic and technical device information for ad serving and fraud prevention:

  • Advertising ID (GAID / AAID): A user-resettable mobile identifier used by Google to serve ads.
  • Technical Device Info: Device manufacturer, OS version, language settings, and approximate region based on IP address.
  • Ad Performance Data: Ad views, clicks, and basic diagnostic telemetry.

To learn more about how Google handles advertising data, please review:

4. How to Control or Reset Ads

You can manage, reset, or opt out of personalized advertisements at any time directly through your Android device settings:

  • Go to Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).
  • Select "Reset advertising ID" or "Delete advertising ID".

You can also visit Google's online settings page: Google Ads Settings.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Arrow Swipe is intended for general audiences and casual puzzle players. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe any personal data has been inadvertently provided, please notify us and we will promptly remove it.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published directly on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage players to review this policy periodically.

Contact & Support

For questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy or Arrow Swipe, please contact us directly via our Google Play Store developer listing page.
